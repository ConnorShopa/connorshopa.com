Habbot Support

Habbot for iPhone

Habbot is a simple way to time your habits. Each habbot is a pair of dots: set a time goal and a tag, tap start, and the dots stretch apart as the time passes. Reach your goal and they fill in.

Common questions

Why are some of my habbots outlined? An outlined habbot ended before its time goal. Filled dots mean the goal was reached.

How do I finish a habbot? Tap anywhere on the dark screen while it's running.

Can I pick up where I left off? Long-press a habbot that fell short of its goal and tap continue. It carries on from where it stopped.

How do I delete a habbot? Long-press it, tap the trash button in its corner, then confirm.

How do I find old habbots? Tap the search button next to the + to filter by tag or colour and see the total time.

Are my habbots backed up? They're saved on your phone only, so deleting Habbot removes them.
