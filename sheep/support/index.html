Sheep : Counting Words for iPhone

Sheep is a calm, endless word game. It starts with one word, sheep. For each of its letters, find a real word that starts with it. When you've used every letter, your last word becomes the new word, and the chain keeps going.

Common questions

Tapping a word says "No content found". Your iPhone doesn't have a dictionary downloaded yet. Go to Settings → General → Dictionary and tap New Oxford American Dictionary (or Oxford Dictionary of English).

Definitions show Apple or Xcode terms. In Settings → General → Dictionary, untick Apple Dictionary.

What does Skip do? It picks a word for the current letter so you can keep going.

Is my progress backed up? Progress is saved on your phone only, so deleting Sheep resets it.
