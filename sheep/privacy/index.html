Sheep Privacy Policy

Sheep : Counting Words · Last updated November 7, 2026

Sheep does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Your progress stays on your phone

Your score, your current word and the words you've found are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Sheep has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Dictionary lookups

When you tap a word, Sheep opens Apple's built-in dictionary on your device. That lookup is handled by iOS.

Deleting your data

Deleting Sheep from your phone removes all of its data.
